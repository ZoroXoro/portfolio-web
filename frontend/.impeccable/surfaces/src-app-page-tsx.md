# Surface brief: home (/)

Scope: the public portfolio home and its project detail pages (/work/[id]); certificates route inherits. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: hiring managers first, prospective clients second (PRODUCT.md). Action: open a project, contact, or download the resume. Proof: the four admin-published projects with real screenshots, the resume, certificates. Constraint: projects come from the admin API, never a repo data file.

Memorable moment: the viridian ZORO seal, which marks only verified-live work and stamps once when a message is delivered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an appraisal sheet for a maker's work. Each project is an attested specimen at full size with its facts ruled beneath it. It refuses the category default of a dark gradient hero plus a bento grid of identical cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white sheet (#F7F8F6), graphite ink (#16181A), slate secondary (#5C6166), hairline rules (#D6D9D6), and one viridian seal (#0F7A55) used only to mean verified-live. Archivo is the only family: expanded black for the maker's mark, normal width for reading, condensed caps for field labels, with tabular figures. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows, no glass, no gradients, no boxes.

STORY: In one screen the visitor learns that Zoro builds full products for real clients across web, mobile and backend, and is extending into LLM systems. They scan the specimens, open one, believe it because every fact is attested, and then contact him or take the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The ZORO mark sits wide at the top left at about 40% of the measure. Beside or under it is a two-line statement, followed by a ruled block (location, availability, focus). The primary actions (Contact, Resume) are a pair of ruled text buttons under the statement. Specimen No. 01's screenshot already crosses the fold at the bottom, with its seal showing.

FORM: Appraisal Sheet, position 7 on the ordered list, seed key 6985740f. Raises: nixie (one colour, live only), cracktro (nothing enclosed), album (fixed catalogue numbers). Signature interaction: specimen rows expand into their detail page with the screenshot carrying continuity, plus the seal stamp on delivery. Motion grammar: short opacity/translate settles, at most once per section, with springs only on the stamp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
